Privacy Policy

Effective 14 July 2026

This policy explains how Kairoz Corporation handles information through Marketing Agent at agenticgrowthlabs.com.

1. Who operates Marketing Agent

Marketing Agent is developed and operated by Kairoz Corporation. Kairoz provides consulting, analytics, automation, and business-technology services. Questions about this policy may be sent to info@kairozcorporation.com.

2. Information we process

Depending on an authorized engagement, Marketing Agent may process business-profile information, campaign objectives, landing-page content, lead-submission information, first-party application events, Google Search Console metrics, Google Analytics metrics, and Google Ads campaign and performance information.

3. Google Ads and Google account data

Google Ads data is accessed only for accounts whose owner or authorized administrator has granted Kairoz Corporation the required access. We use this data to provide campaign reporting, validation, approved campaign creation, and approved campaign-management services. We do not sell Google Ads data, use it for unrelated advertising, or make it available to another client.

Users may revoke Google account access through their Google account security settings or by asking Kairoz to disconnect the relevant account.

4. How information is used

  • Deliver the agreed consulting, reporting, content, analytics, and campaign-management services.
  • Validate and execute explicitly approved marketing operations.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and create an operational audit trail.
  • Improve the reliability and usability of the product without exposing client-confidential information.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure and API providers that are necessary to operate the service, including cloud hosting and authorized Google services. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of the service.

6. Security and access controls

Production credentials are maintained in restricted secret storage. Application data and actions are separated by business or campaign identifiers, and material publishing or advertising operations require authorization. No security system can guarantee absolute protection, but Kairoz applies administrative and technical safeguards proportionate to the service.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the authorized engagement, maintain operational and security records, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Account owners may request deletion or disconnection by contacting us. Residual copies may remain temporarily in secured backups or where retention is legally required.

8. Your choices

Subject to applicable law and the relevant engagement, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of their personal information. Requests should identify the relevant company or campaign so we can verify authority before acting.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the product, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest published version.
